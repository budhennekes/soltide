    Why daylight matters

    Your circadian rhythm is your body’s daily clock. Light helps set when you feel awake and when you feel sleepy.

    Take your morning outside.

    Make outdoor light part of your morning. A walk or coffee outside is a good place to start.

    Give it a regular spot.

    Pair daylight with something you already do, like breakfast or walking the dog.

    Let the evening get darker.

    Dim bright lights before bed. Evening light can shift your body clock toward a later bedtime.

    Never look directly at the sun. Use sun protection outdoors.

    About this app

    Sunrise and sunset times are estimates, shown in your device’s timezone. The timeline shows time, not UV or light intensity.

    Your location and logged time stay in this browser. There’s no account or cloud backup. Clearing browser data deletes your records.
